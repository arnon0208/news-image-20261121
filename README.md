# The Daily Globe — Saturday 21 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Saturday 21 November
2026, drawn entirely in code: the Basra power station fire, the magnitude 6.9
quake in Peru's Andes, Bitcoin breaking below $60,000, the Manipur toxic-liquor
toll, Salvadoran troops killed in Haiti, and the Kryvyi Rih strike toll.

`draw.py` (Python + Pillow) renders `entry.png` (1024×1024). `main.ts` copies
it to `/output/entry.png`. All facts were verified against reports published
on 21 November 2026 — see `NOTES.md` for sources and for the stories
deliberately left out.
